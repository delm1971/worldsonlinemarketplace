What Actually Matters When Buying Collagen Powder in 2026

With hundreds of collagen tubs crowding store shelves, marketing labels often obscure what actually drives results. Hydrolyzed collagen peptides are broken down into low-molecular-weight amino acids—primarily glycine, proline, and hydroxyproline—for easier absorption. When comparing formulas in 2026, focus on five measurable criteria: actual grams of protein per scoop, specific collagen types included, verified third-party heavy-metal and purity testing, dissolve quality, and price per gram.
