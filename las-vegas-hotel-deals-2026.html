Midweek vs. Weekend Pricing & Resort Fee Traps

Lodging is the single largest expense of a Las Vegas vacation—and also the easiest cost to cut. In 2026, Sunday through Thursday room rates on the Las Vegas Strip routinely drop 55% to 70% below Friday and Saturday rates. Always check the final checkout total, as mandatory daily resort fees ($39–$55 per night plus tax) can double the cost of a "$35 teaser rate."

12 Proven Ways to Score Las Vegas Hotel Deals in 2026

  1. Book Sunday–Wednesday instead of Friday–Sunday: Even shifting your trip by a single night saves hundreds on Strip resorts.
  2. Audit daily resort fees upfront: Compare all-in out-the-door totals rather than advertised base rates.
  3. Join MGM Rewards and Caesars Rewards for free: Instant member discounts apply even with zero casino play.
  4. Bundle flights + hotel together: Package booking engines unlock wholesale hotel rates hidden from standalone searches.
  5. Check the Las Vegas convention calendar: Avoid weeks with 100,000+ trade show attendees unless traveling for business.
  6. Consider off-Strip boutique & condo-style hotels: Properties one block off Las Vegas Blvd often waive resort fees and parking.
  7. Book refundable rates and re-price weekly: Vegas hotel revenue algorithms adjust daily based on occupancy.
  8. Target shoulder travel windows: Post-CES January, late August, and early December offer peak room inventory deals.
  9. Look for dining & beverage credit promos: Many 2026 direct promos include $50–$100 daily food credits.
  10. Compare Downtown Las Vegas properties: Lower resort fees, walkable attractions, and cheaper dining.
  11. Stack credit card travel portals: Combine annual travel credits with member rates.
  12. Use a dedicated hotel comparison directory: Cross-check multiple booking engines simultaneously before locking in.
