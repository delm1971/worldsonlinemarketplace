Sample Prompts & Beginner Workflow Tips

"Create a 45-second vertical 9:16 explainer video for small business owners showing 3 ways to automate customer follow-ups. Use a warm, energetic voiceover, bold dynamic captions, and clean modern workspace B-roll."
  • Define Aspect Ratio & Length Upfront: Always state 9:16 (TikTok/Reels/Shorts) or 16:9 (YouTube) and target duration in sentence one.
  • Write Short, Conversational Sentences: Sentences under 15 words produce far more natural AI voiceover cadence and cleaner on-screen subtitles.
  • Batch Generate Hooks First: Test 3 different opening 5-second visual hooks for social ads before rendering full-length variations.
