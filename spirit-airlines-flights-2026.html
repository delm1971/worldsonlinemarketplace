How Spirit Airlines Base Fares, Bag Fees & Seat Charges Work

Spirit Airlines unbundles every component of air travel so travelers only pay for what they use. Your base ticket includes transportation plus one personal item (such as a backpack or laptop tote up to 18" x 14" x 8") that fits beneath the seat in front of you. Overhead carry-on bags, checked luggage, and specific seat assignments cost extra—and prices climb sharply the closer you wait until boarding.

How to Find the Real Total Cost Before Booking

  • Run the All-In Round-Trip Comparison: Add your required bag and seat fees to Spirit's base fare first, then compare that exact figure against standard economy on legacy airlines.
  • Check Ticket Bundles vs. À La Carte: If you want both a carry-on and seat selection, Spirit's bundled fare tiers often beat adding each perk separately.
  • Book at the Airport Ticket Counter: Buying in person at the airport counter waives Spirit's online Passenger Usage Charge ($22.99+ per segment) on qualifying flights.
  • Travel Light for 2–4 Day Trips: Mastering the free personal item turns a $39–$59 flash sale fare into unbeatable value.
