Top Platforms for Finding Remote VA Jobs

1. Dedicated Virtual Assistant Agencies

Agencies screen clients and match you directly with long-term opportunities, taking away the pressure of pitching from scratch.

  • Belay Solutions: Premier US-based agency matching executive VAs with corporate clients and non-profits.
  • Time etc: Great for administrative professionals seeking flexible, part-time virtual assistant roles.
  • Fancy Hands: Fast-paced entry-level site for micro-tasks and short-form administrative support.

2. Freelance Marketplaces & Remote Job Boards

Direct-to-client platforms allow you to set your own rates and build a direct client portfolio.

  • Upwork & Fiverr: Ideal for building an initial portfolio, gaining verified reviews, and testing different VA service packages.
  • FlexJobs: A vetted remote job board free of scams and low-quality listings.
  • We Work Remotely: Top job board featuring full-time and contract remote operations roles worldwide.

Essential Skills Needed for Virtual Assistants

To stand out to hiring managers and business owners, master these foundational tools and soft skills:

  • Communication & Organization: Clear written English, inbox organization (Gmail, Outlook), and proactive follow-ups.
  • Calendar & Scheduling Tools: Calendly, Google Calendar, and Acuity Scheduling.
  • Project Management Software: Asana, Trello, Notion, and Monday.com.
  • Basic Content Creation: Canva for quick social banners, basic photo editing, and PDF creation.

How to Land Remote VA Jobs with No Prior Experience

  1. Repackage Past Experience: Translate your past experience in customer service, office management, retail, or administration into virtual assistance skills (e.g., "customer communication" becomes "inbox management").
  2. Offer a Free Beta Trial: Reach out to 2–3 small business owners or local non-profits and offer 5–10 hours of free support in exchange for a glowing testimonial and case study.
  3. Create a Simple One-Page Portfolio: Use Google Docs, Notion, or Canva to outline your service offerings, tools you know, and testimonial quotes.
  4. Optimize Your LinkedIn Profile: Update your headline to "Virtual Assistant helping [Target Audience] streamline operations & save 10+ hours/week."
