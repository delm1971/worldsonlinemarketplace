How to Find Budget Airline Deals in 2026

How to catch flash fare sales, stack promo codes, and dodge hidden bag and seat charges on domestic and international budget airlines.

Fare Sale Timing & Alert Setup

  • Join Low-Cost Carrier Email Lists: Airlines like Spirit, Frontier, Allegiant, Breeze, Play, and ZIPAIR release 24- to 48-hour promo codes midweek before third-party search engines reflect the discount.
  • Search Off-Peak Midweek Dates: Flash sale promo codes typically apply to Tuesday, Wednesday, and Saturday departures—avoiding Friday/Sunday surcharges.
  • Set Route-Specific Price Alerts: Track your target city pairs 6–10 weeks ahead for domestic trips and 3–5 months ahead for international low-cost routes.
