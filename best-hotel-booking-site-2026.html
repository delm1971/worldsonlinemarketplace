When Booking Direct Beats Booking Through a Site

Book directly with the hotel brand whenever you want elite night credits, guaranteed room-type requests, or spot a lower public rate on an OTA—major hotel chains match identical third-party rates and take an extra 10% to 25% off under their Best Rate Guarantees.
