1. Take in Breathtaking Skyline Views

SUMMIT One Vanderbilt

Spanning three floors of mirrors, floating spheres, and floor-to-ceiling glass sky boxes directly above Grand Central Terminal, SUMMIT is NYC's most immersive observation experience.

Empire State Building Observatory & Museum

No trip to NYC is complete without ascending the world's most famous skyscraper. Explore nine floors of interactive historical exhibits before stepping onto the open-air 86th-floor observatory.

Edge at Hudson Yards

Protruding 80 feet out into the sky from the 100th floor, Edge is the highest outdoor sky deck in the Western Hemisphere. Stand on the transparent glass floor and look 1,100 feet straight down to the city streets below.

Top of the Rock at Rockefeller Center

Offering three tiers of indoor and outdoor viewing decks, Top of the Rock provides the absolute best view of Central Park to the north and the Empire State Building to the south.

2. Explore NYC Neighborhoods & Free Attractions

  • Stroll Central Park: Walk across Bow Bridge, visit Bethesda Terrace, and explore Ramble woodland trails in the heart of Manhattan.
  • Walk the Brooklyn Bridge: Enjoy sweeping views of Lower Manhattan and the East River by crossing this iconic suspension bridge at sunrise or dusk.
  • Discover the High Line & Chelsea Market: Walk along an elevated, transformed freight line park before grabbing artisanal bites at Chelsea Market.
  • Statue of Liberty & Ellis Island: Take the official ferry out to Liberty Island to inspect Lady Liberty up close and explore immigration history at Ellis Island.
  • Catch a Broadway Show: Head to the Theater District to witness world-class musicals and drama performances in person.

Top Tips for Visiting New York City in 2026

  1. Book Major Attractions early: High-demand observation decks (SUMMIT, Edge) and Statue of Liberty crown passes require advance online booking.
  2. Use the Subway: OMNY tap-to-pay makes riding the NYC subway effortless—simply tap your contactless card or mobile wallet at turnstiles.
  3. Wear Comfortable Shoes: Expect to walk 10,000–20,000 steps per day while exploring Manhattan and Brooklyn.
